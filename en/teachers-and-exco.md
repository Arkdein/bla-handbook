## The SOP and this handbook

- **The SOP is the authority.** The Member's Guide summarises it for members. If the two differ, the SOP applies, and the Member's Guide should be corrected.
- **When the SOP changes,** tell the handbook owner so that the matching section of the Member's Guide, in both languages, can be updated.
- **Do not copy the SOP onto this site.** The handbook is public; the SOP stays where access can be controlled.

## EXCO access

EXCO members can view the SOP document itself, but not the teachers' records folder. There are two exceptions. All EXCO members are editors of the BLA excusal form and its response sheet: the Vice-President (Events and Admin) leads, adding each activity to the form before the notice goes out and handling the handover, retention and clean-up of these records, and the rest of the EXCO cover when the Vice-President is unavailable. The Vice-President (Events and Admin) can also edit that year's attendance record (SOP 8). Every EXCO member keeps students' reasons confidential. The excusal form is owned by a teacher, so it stays with BLA when the EXCO changes. At each handover, teachers remove the outgoing EXCO's access to the SOP and give it to the incoming EXCO.
