## BLA Lecture Series 1: AI and the US–China Rivalry {#lecture-1-2027-01-25}

**Tech competition, shifting US politics, and Southeast Asia's strategic choices**

The lecture is in Chinese. Chinese title: 《AI时代的中美大国博弈：科技竞争、美国政治变局与东南亚的战略选择》.

| | |
|:--|:--|
| **Speaker** | Assoc Prof Li Mingjiang, S. Rajaratnam School of International Studies (RSIS), Nanyang Technological University |
| **Date** | Monday 25 January 2027 |
| **Time** | 4.20 pm to 6 pm |
| **Venue** | To be confirmed |
| **Language** | Chinese |
| **Format** | A talk of about 60 minutes, then questions and discussion |
| **For** | Sec 4 and JC2 students taking the Bicultural Studies Programme (BSP) and China Studies in Chinese (CSC) |

The lecture looks at US politics from the 2026 midterms to the 2028 presidential election, and covers four areas:

1. **US politics and China tech policy:** where the two US parties agree and disagree on tech competition with China.
2. **The US–China AI race:** how AI, advanced chips and home-grown innovation shape the rivalry and global tech supply chains.
3. **Tech competition and world order:** whether the global tech system will split faster, and whether there is still room to work together on AI safety and governance.
4. **What it means for Singapore and ASEAN:** balancing economic growth, tech partnerships, national security and strategic autonomy.

{: .note }
The speaker may adjust the topic and content slightly. What is presented on the day applies.
